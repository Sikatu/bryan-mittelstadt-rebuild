# Client Content and Asset Checklist

This checklist contains everything required to complete the Bryan Mittelstadt website without guessing or publishing unverified information.

## Priority 1 — Launch-Critical

- Approved high-resolution homepage hero image.
- Approved primary acting reel YouTube or Vimeo link.
- Current résumé PDF.
- Final biography.
- Current direct-contact preference.
- Confirmed representation agency, agent, phone, email, and website details.
- IMDb, Instagram, YouTube, and any other public profile URLs.
- 1200 × 630 social-sharing image or approved source image for its creation.

## Priority 2 — Portfolio Media

- Acting project stills or posters with project names and usage approval.
- Voice-over commercial reel.
- Voice-over narration or radio-drama reels.
- *Darling* album artwork.
- Music release date or approved wording when the date is not public.
- Streaming, pre-save, purchase, performance, or press links.
- Writing, directing, and producing project list.
- Logline, role, status, year, and approved public link for each filmmaking project.

## Priority 3 — Casting Materials

- Theatrical headshots.
- Commercial headshots.
- Preferred display order and labels.
- High-resolution originals approved for download.
- Optional ZIP archive for casting teams.

## Required Confirmations

- Exact title and status of *The Overview Effect*.
- Exact relationship between *Arabesque* and *Blood and Sex Over Ambition*.
- Approved wording for the Durango Film Festival award.
- Festival selections and awards that may be displayed publicly.
- Union affiliations and preferred formatting.
- Physical details for casting use.
- Whether agent contact details should be public or available only by request.
- Whether the contact page should use direct email, a form, or both.
- Whether mailing-list signup is managed through Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Substack, another provider, or not yet selected.

## File Delivery Standard

- Images: original JPG, PNG, TIFF, or high-quality WebP files.
- Reel and audio: public URLs or original files with explicit hosting approval.
- Documents: final PDF files, not screenshots.
- Naming: include the project or purpose in each filename.
- Rights: confirm Bryan owns the asset or has permission to publish it.
