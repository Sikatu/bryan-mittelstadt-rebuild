### 2. Bio Page
**Text Content:**
> Bryan is an actor, vo artist, and singer in Los Angeles.
> He is about to lead The Overview Effect, a new musical alongside Sasheer Zamata (SNL, Agatha All Along). It will be a filmed series as well as be staged at the Disney Hall's Redcat Theatre.
> He just won Best Actor at the 21st annual Durango Film Festival for the feature, Quiet After Supper.
> 
> He also led, Julius, which premiered at the esteemed Torino Film Festival, led acclaimed director's Pan Luo upcoming piece, Arabesque, as Van Gogh and Sundance nominated director, Jeff Lipsky's Goldilocks and the Two Bears.
> Bryan grew up in Texas where he completed his 75 page thesis on the history and trajectory of film noir, trained operatically in New York until finally returning to his love of acting and film. After completing his MFA in Acting in 2020. Bryan moved to Los Angeles and has since worked on dozens of films, many noted in Rio, Sehsuechte, Mumbai, Aesthetica, deadCenter, Torino, SXSW, and more. When not in front of the camera, Bryan is completing his upcoming folk album and scripts, playing guitar, or traveling.
> Bryan is committed to creating a more inclusive artistic environment and is interested in exploring inequalities and social justice issues in overt and subtle ways.
